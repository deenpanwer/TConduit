# Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (PIIA)

![Company Logo]({{ORG_LOGO}})

This Proprietary Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between **{{ORG_NAME}}** (the "Company") and **{{CANDIDATE_NAME}}** (the "Employee").

## 1. Confidential Information
Employee understands that during employment with the Company, they will have access to Confidential Information. Employee agrees to hold all such Confidential Information in strictest confidence and not to disclose, use, or publish any Confidential Information without the prior written consent of the Company, except as necessary to perform their duties.

## 2. Assignment of Inventions
### 2.1 Definition
"Inventions" means all discoveries, developments, designs, improvements, inventions, formulas, software programs, processes, techniques, know-how, and data, whether or not patentable or registrable under copyright or similar statutes, made or conceived or reduced to practice or learned by Employee, either alone or jointly with others, during the period of employment.

### 2.2 Assignment
Employee hereby assigns and agrees to assign in the future to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to all Inventions defined in Section 2.1.

### 2.3 Exception
This assignment does not apply to an Invention for which no equipment, supplies, facility, or trade secret information of the Company was used and which was developed entirely on Employee’s own time, unless (a) the Invention relates to the business of the Company, or (b) the Invention results from any work performed by Employee for the Company.

## 3. Moral Rights
To the extent allowed by law, Employee hereby waives all moral rights in the Inventions and works of authorship created hereunder.

---

## 4. Return of Company Property
Upon termination of employment or at the request of the Company, Employee shall immediately deliver to the Company all materials, documents, equipment, and other property belonging to the Company.

## 5. Non-Solicitation
During the term of employment and for a period of one (1) year thereafter, Employee agrees not to solicit any employee or independent contractor of the Company to leave their employment or engagement.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of **{{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE}}**.
