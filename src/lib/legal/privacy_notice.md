# Privacy Notice

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Trac AI, Inc. ("Trac AI", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.trac.ai (the "Site"), use our platform, or otherwise engage with us.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us
*   **Account Information:** Name, email address, password, job title, and company name when you register.
*   **Hiring Data:** Information about candidates, job descriptions, and offer terms you input into the Service.
*   **Payment Information:** Billing address and credit card details (processed by our third-party payment processors).

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically
*   **Usage Data:** We collect information about your interaction with our Services, such as pages visited, features used, and time spent.
*   **Device Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
*   **Cookies and Tracking:** We use cookies, pixels, and local storage to recognize you and customize your experience.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
1.  **Providing the Service:** To operate, maintain, and improve our hiring platform.
2.  **Communication:** To send you updates, security alerts, and support messages.
3.  **Analytics:** To understand user behavior and trends to improve our product.
4.  **Legal Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests.

## 4. Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:
*   **Service Providers:** Third-party vendors who provide hosting, email, analytics, and payment services.
*   **Affiliates:** Companies within our corporate family.
*   **Legal Authorities:** If required by law or to protect our rights.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, or as required by law.

## 6. Your Data Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
*   Access the personal data we hold about you.
*   Request correction of inaccurate data.
*   Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten").
*   Object to processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact privacy@trac.ai.

Trac AI is headquartered in the United States. Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries. We use approved transfer mechanisms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your data.
