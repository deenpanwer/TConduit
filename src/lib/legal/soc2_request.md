# SOC 2 Request

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

## Request Access to Our Security Reports

Trac AI maintains a SOC 2 Type II report demonstrating our commitment to security, availability, and confidentiality.

Due to the sensitive nature of these reports, access is restricted to:
*   Current Enterprise Customers
*   Prospective Enterprise Customers (under NDA)

### How to Request
To request a copy of our latest SOC 2 report, please email **security@trac.ai** with the subject line "SOC 2 Report Request".

Please include:
1.  Your Name
2.  Your Company Name
3.  Reason for Request

Our security team will review your request and, if approved, send you a secure link to download the report.
