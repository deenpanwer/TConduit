# GDPR Compliance

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

Trac AI is fully committed to compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Our Role
*   **Processor:** For the candidate data you upload to our platform, you are the Controller and we are the Processor.
*   **Controller:** For your account registration data, we are the Controller.

## 2. Data Subject Rights
We support all GDPR data subject rights, including:
*   Right to Access
*   Right to Rectification
*   Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
*   Right to Portability

## 3. International Transfers
We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure legal grounds for data transfers outside the EEA. See our Data Processing Agreement (DPA) for details.

## 4. Subprocessors
We maintain a list of all subprocessors. See our Subprocessors page for details.
