# Data Residency

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

## 1. Default Region
Unless otherwise configured, all customer data is stored in our primary region: **US East (N. Virginia)**.

## 2. EU Data Residency
For Enterprise customers, we offer the option to store data exclusively within the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

## 3. Data Processing Locations
While data at rest may be pinned to a specific region, data processing (e.g., by our AI models) may occur in other regions depending on service availability and performance requirements, subject to strict security controls and our DPA.

## 4. Requesting Specific Residency
To discuss data residency options for your organization, please contact our sales team.
