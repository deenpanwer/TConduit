# Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA)

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

## 1. Overview
In accordance with the *Schrems II* decision by the Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU), Trac AI conducts Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) to evaluate the risks associated with transferring personal data to countries outside the EEA ("Third Countries").

## 2. Assessment Methodology
Our TIA process involves:
1.  **Mapping Transfers:** Identifying all data flows to Third Countries.
2.  **Evaluating Laws:** Assessing the legal framework of the Third Country regarding public authority access to data (e.g., FISA 702 in the US).
3.  **Identifying Safeguards:** Determining the technical, organizational, and contractual measures in place to protect the data.

## 3. US Transfer Assessment
For transfers to the United States, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where applicable) or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) supplemented by additional safeguards:
*   **Encryption At Rest:** Data is encrypted ensuring that even if accessed, it is unintelligible.
*   **Encryption In Transit:** TLS 1.2+ for all data in motion.
*   **Access Controls:** Strict strict need-to-know access policies.

## 4. Conclusion
Based on our assessment and the supplementary measures implemented, we conclude that the transfer of data to the US provides an essentially equivalent level of protection to that guaranteed within the EEA.
