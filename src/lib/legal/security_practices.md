# Security Practices

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

At Trac AI, security is not an afterthought; it is integral to our operations. We implement rigorous technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

## 1. Compliance & Certifications

### 1.1 SOC 2 Type II
Trac AI is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Our controls are audited annually by an independent third-party firm. The report covers the Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, and Confidentiality.

### 1.2 GDPR & CCPA
We are fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). See our Privacy Notice for more details.

## 2. Technical Security Measures

### 2.1 Encryption
*   **In Transit:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
*   **At Rest:** Data stored in our databases and file systems is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.

### 2.2 Access Control
*   **Least Privilege:** Access to production systems is granted on a strict need-to-know basis.
*   **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is enforced for all employee access to internal systems and cloud infrastructure.

### 2.3 Vulnerability Management
*   **Penetration Testing:** We engage third-party security firms to conduct annual penetration tests.
*   **Scanning:** We perform automated vulnerability scanning of our code and infrastructure daily.

## 3. Organizational Security

### 3.1 Employee Training
All employees undergo mandatory security awareness training upon hire and annually thereafter. This includes training on phishing, password security, and data handling.

### 3.2 Incident Response
We maintain a formal Incident Response Plan (IRP) that outlines procedures for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents. The IRP is tested at least annually.

### 3.3 Vendor Management
We perform due diligence on all third-party vendors to ensure they meet our security standards before engaging them.

## 4. Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity
*   **Backups:** We perform automated daily backups of all customer data. Backups are encrypted and stored in a separate geographic region.
*   **Testing:** Disaster recovery procedures are tested annually to ensure we can restore operations within our target Recovery Time Objective (RTO).

## 5. Reporting Security Issues
If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Trac AI, please report it to us immediately at security@trac.ai. We operate a responsible disclosure program.
